How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Every level is a tangle of arrows with birds locked in cages. Pick the right order and the whole board unravels.

1

Tap an arrow

It slides out in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps back.

2

Clear the path

Each cage has its own door and a coloured escape lane. Empty the lane to open it.

3

Free every bird

Free them all to win. No bumps earns three stars, and every first clear earns coins.
