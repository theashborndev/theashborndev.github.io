Arrow Bird Escape – Privacy Policy

Developer: MHS Solutions · Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Arrow Bird Escape (package com.mhssolutions.arrowbirdescape, "the app") handles and why. We have kept data use to the minimum needed to run the game and show optional ads.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat. We (MHS Solutions) do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information, and we run no servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, best times) and settings (theme, sound, music) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example, to get a hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings),
  • IP address, device model, operating system and app version,
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched).

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the game.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads for all apps in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Watching ads is always optional; the game can be played without them.

Game engine

The app is built with Unity. The engine may report technical crash or performance data needed to operate, which does not identify you personally. See the Unity privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

We do not hold any of your data. Data handled by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
